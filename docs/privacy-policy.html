Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Your privacy matters. Facebook Tweaks is designed to customise the Facebook interface without collecting or transmitting your personal information.

1. Information Collection

Facebook Tweaks does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or user data with the developer or third parties.

2. Local Storage

The extension stores your interface preferences locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API. These settings control which Facebook interface elements are displayed or hidden. They are not transmitted to external servers.

3. Website Access

Facebook Tweaks accesses the Facebook website to identify and customise selected visual interface elements according to your preferences. It does not collect Facebook messages, passwords, or account information.

4. Third-Party Services

The extension does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or privacy practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or the extension, please contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store support page.
